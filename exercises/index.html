The warm-up

Eight minutes, every session, no exceptions. It is not cardio and it is not optional.

  • Easy cardio. A bike, a rower or a brisk walk.3 to 5 minGet warm
  • Leg swings, forwards and back then side to side10 eachOpen the hips up
  • Cat and cow8 repsWake the back up one bit at a time
  • Band pull-apart15 repsSwitch the upper back on
  • Bodyweight squat, as deep as is comfortable10 repsGroove the movement

Back Squat

4 x 6–8Effort 7–8

Gym
As named above. The main version.
Home
Backpack squat, heels on a book
Joints
Goblet squat or box squat
Set up
Bar resting on the muscle across the back of your shoulders. Feet a little wider than your hips, toes turned out slightly.
The way down
Sit straight down between your hips. Three seconds. Chest stays up.
How far
Hip crease below the top of your knee. Stop where your back is still flat.
The usual mistake
Knees falling inwards as you stand up. Push them out against the floor.

Gym
As named above. The main version.
Home
Backpack split squat
Joints
Leg press, shorter range
Set up
Feet flat and about hip width, in the middle of the plate. Back and hips pressed into the seat.
The way down
Lower it under control until your knees come near your chest.
How far
As deep as you can go before your hips start to lift off the seat.
The usual mistake
Snapping your knees straight at the top. Stop just short of locked.

Barbell Bench Press

4 x 6–8Effort 7–8

Gym
As named above. The main version.
Home
Push-up with your feet up and a backpack on
Joints
Dumbbell press, palms facing in
Set up
Shoulder blades squeezed together and pulled down. Feet planted.
The way down
Two to three seconds down to your lower chest, elbows at about 45 degrees.
How far
Touch your chest lightly. Never bounce it off.
The usual mistake
Elbows flaring straight out to the sides. The quickest route to a sore shoulder.

As named above. The main version.
Home

Joints
Landmine press
Set up
Bar resting on your front shoulders. Ribs down, glutes tight, feet under your hips.
The way down
Back down to your collarbone under control. Move your head out of the way, then back through.

Lock out with your arms beside your ears.
The usual mistake
Leaning back to get it up. If your lower back arches, it is too heavy.

Three seconds down to a dead hang, every rep.

Romanian Deadlift

4 x 8–10Effort 7–8

Gym
As named above. The main version.
Home
One-leg version holding a backpack
Joints
Dumbbell version, shorter range
Set up
Bar against your thighs. Knees slightly bent, then locked at that angle for every rep.
The way down
Push your hips back and let the bar slide down your legs. Slow is the point.
How far
Stop when your hamstrings run out of stretch or your back starts to round. Usually mid shin.
The usual mistake
Turning it into a squat. Your knees do not travel forward at any point.

Ten minutes after every session

Stretching

Stretch warm, never cold. The end of a session is the right time for it.

  • Hip stretch, back knee down and back foot up on a chair. Each side.45 secThe front of the hip, which sitting and squatting both shorten
  • Sitting hip rotations, one leg in front and one out to the side8 each sideTurning the hip in and out
  • Seated hamstring stretch, each side45 secThe back of the thigh after hinging
  • Upper back stretch over a bench or a rolled towel8 repsThe upper back, which decides how far overhead you can reach
  • Chest stretch in a doorway, each side45 secThe chest after pressing
  • Child’s pose, reaching over to one side45 sec each sideThe lats and lower back

Days off. Run the list above twice, then add these three.

  • Deep squat hold with your heels flat60 to 90 secAnkles, knees and hips at full range
  • Slow side-to-side squat8 each sideInner thigh and the side of the hip
  • Hang from a bar30 to 45 secTakes the pressure off your shoulders and builds grip
