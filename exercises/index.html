The warm-up

Eight minutes. Ten on the 40+ track. It is not optional and it is not cardio.

  • Easy cardio — bike, row, or brisk walk3–5 minRaise core temperature
  • Leg swings, front to back and side to side10 eachOpen the hips
  • Cat–cow8 repsWake the spine up segment by segment
  • Band pull-apart15 repsUpper back on, shoulders set
  • Bodyweight squat to a comfortable depth10 repsGroove the pattern

Back Squat

4 × 6–8RPE 7–8

Gym
As named — the main version.
Home
Backpack squat, heels on a book
40+
Goblet or box squat
Set up
Bar on the rear delts, feet just outside hip width, toes turned out a few degrees.
Lower it
Sit down between the hips, not back — three seconds down, chest staying proud.
Range
Hip crease below the top of the knee. Stop where you can still hold a flat back.
Most common error
Knees collapsing inward out of the hole. Push them out against the floor as you stand.

Romanian Deadlift

3 × 8–10RPE 7

Gym
As named — the main version.
Home
Single-leg RDL holding a backpack
40+
Dumbbell RDL, shorter range
Set up
Bar against the thighs, knees softly bent — then locked at that angle for every rep.
Lower it
Push the hips back and let the bar drag down the leg. Slow is the point.
Range
Stop when the hamstrings run out of stretch or the back starts to round. Usually mid-shin.
Most common error
Turning it into a squat. The knees do not travel forward at any point.

Home

40+
Seated calf raise if balance is poor
Set up
Balls of the feet on the platform, knees straight and staying straight.

Two seconds down into a deep stretch. No bouncing out of the bottom.

After every session — ten minutes

Mobility & stretching

Stretch warm, never cold. The end of a session is the right time.

  • Couch stretch, each side45 sHip flexors, shortened by sitting and by squatting
  • 90/90 hip rotation8 / sideInternal and external hip rotation
  • Seated hamstring stretch, each side45 sHamstrings after hinging
  • Thoracic extension over a bench or foam roller8 repsUpper back, which decides your overhead reach
  • Doorway pec stretch, each side45 sChest after pressing
  • Child’s pose with a side reach45 s / sideLats and lower back

Rest days — run the list above twice, then add these three

  • Deep squat hold, heels down60–90 sAnkles, knees and hips at end range
  • Cossack squat, slow8 / sideAdductors and lateral hip
  • Dead hang from a bar30–45 sDecompression, shoulders and grip
